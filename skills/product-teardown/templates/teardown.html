How Example App turns a sentence into an illustrated story

I watched one real story being made in a logged-in session for 10 minutes. I recorded the app's web requests (field names only) and every website it fetched files from.

Seen means it showed up in the recording. Best guess means it's inferred from clues.

The big picture

The browser sends your idea to Example App's server. AI services there write the story, draw the pictures and record the narration, then hand back a "recipe" plus file links. Your browser shows the result.

Step by step, as the page does it

  1. You type your idea

    The page checks your credits before anything else.

    Gets back: credits, plan

  2. You press create (the main call)

    One request, POST /stories, sends your idea and gets back a job id.

    Sends: prompt, style, language · Gets back: job_id

  3. The waiting screen

    The page asks "is it ready yet?" every few seconds (/jobs/{id}) until status says done.

  4. The story loads

    It fetches the finished story, then the images and audio for each page from the file server.

    Gets back: pages[].text, pages[].image_url, pages[].audio_url

Which AI is likely behind each part

The browser never talked to an AI company directly, so these are inferred from clues.

Story writer

Best guess

A large language model. The clue is a single long wait on the main call before any text appears.

Illustrator

Best guess

An image-generation model. The images arrive one by one, each with its own job status.

Narrator

Best guess

A text-to-speech service. Give the clue that points to a vendor, or say there isn't one.
